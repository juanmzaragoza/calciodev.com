## Context

Listing Los Cabos is a real estate platform in Mexico. When they came to us, the product had been inherited from previous developers — a React + Firebase application that was only half-built, with key features broken, including property upload and the map.

The deeper problem was ownership: **the client did not own their own source code or hosting account.** Internally, listings were managed with paper and Excel, there was no unified view, and all the knowledge lived in one person's head.

## The problem

- A half-finished platform with critical flows broken.
- No control over the code or the hosting environment — the previous vendor kept it.
- No authentication, no monetization, no single source of truth for listings.

## What we did

- Rebuilt the React application with a proper service layer and a responsive design.
- Moved all keys into environment variables and stood up a **new hosting environment under the client's exclusive control.**
- Added authentication and monetization through **Stripe subscriptions** — three plans with active-listing limits (10 / 30 / 50) and a 60-day free trial.
- Consolidated everything into **one codebase serving two brands/cities** — Los Cabos and Mexico City.

## Result

The client now has full control of their system, a new recurring revenue stream from subscriptions, and a much easier way to publish properties and know their status at a glance.
