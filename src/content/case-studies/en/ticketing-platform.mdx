## Context

A high-volume ticketing platform in Spain needed to ship faster without breaking the flows their revenue depends on — ticket purchase, coupons, check-in, and ticket resending.

## The problem

- No automated tests at all.
- Releases were validated by hand, which was slow and error-prone.
- The most critical flows — purchase, coupons, check-in, ticket resending — had no automation protecting them.

## What we did

- Built a **testing strategy from zero**: backend integration tests plus end-to-end tests with Playwright.
- Added a **post-deploy quality gate** that runs the critical tests after every deployment.
- Tested **three payment gateways end to end**, including 3DS authentication, across three browsers.
- Embedded analytics with per-seller filtering so the business could see its own numbers.

## Result

Release validation went from days to minutes, and the team could ship changes to critical flows without holding their breath.

## Stack

Playwright · Backend integration testing · 3DS payment gateways · Embedded analytics
