## Context

For nearly seven years (2014–2020), as an outsourced provider, we held technical leadership of almost every system we participated in inside the City of Buenos Aires' Ministry of Modernization.

## The problem

Public-sector software carries a different kind of risk: these are systems where errors have real consequences for citizens and public employees, and where traceability is not optional.

## What we did

Led the technical work across a portfolio of systems, including:

- **Public-employee performance evaluation** (in production at evaluaciongcaba.buenosaires.gob.ar).
- **Traceability of subsidized medication** dispensed through pharmacies.
- **Civic participation** platforms.

Across the engagement, that meant **12 systems** over **5 different technology stacks**, with the long-term consistency and documentation that public infrastructure demands.

## Result

A body of work proving that we can take technical ownership of critical systems — where traceability and reliability matter more than moving fast — and sustain them for years.
