## Context

A B2B data platform needed to give each of its customers a view of their own data — dashboards, reports, and AI data agents — without building a separate report for every client.

## The problem

Serving analytics to multiple business customers from one platform means strict isolation: every client must see only their data, and nobody else's. Doing that while keeping a shared, upgradable infrastructure is where most teams get stuck.

## What we did

- Built the **backend in NestJS** (in production since May 2024), with Next.js on the front end and PostgreSQL underneath.
- Added **SSO with several corporate providers** and role-based access control (RBAC).
- Embedded **Apache Superset with per-client isolation**, plus AI data agents.
- Ran a **major migration of the Superset fork** — three major versions (4.1 → 6.1) in 30 days. Automated checks caught three features that would otherwise have been silently lost across ~4,400 merged files.

## Result

Three isolated production tenants, each seeing only their own data, on a platform that can still be upgraded safely.

## Stack

NestJS · Next.js · PostgreSQL · Apache Superset · SSO · RBAC
