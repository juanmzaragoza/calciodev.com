## Context

New Condal is a digital marketing agency in Barcelona. Each month, data from several clients' Google Business Profile accounts had to be pulled together by hand to build reports.

## The problem

The reporting worked, but it depended on someone manually gathering Google Business Profile data across multiple accounts every month — slow, repetitive, and easy to get wrong.

## What we did

- Built a **custom Apps Script connector** that extracts Google Business Profile data (reviews, ratings, visibility) across multiple accounts.
- Fed that data into **Looker Studio dashboards** that turn raw numbers into insights clients can read directly.

## Result

The manual monthly report disappeared. Data flows automatically from multiple accounts into clean dashboards, so the agency can spend its time helping clients grow instead of assembling spreadsheets.

## Stack

Google Apps Script · Google Business Profile · Looker Studio
