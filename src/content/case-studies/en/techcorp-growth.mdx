# The Challenge

TechCorp had an incredible product, but their customer acquisition was almost entirely dependent on paid search (Google Ads and LinkedIn). As competition increased, their CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) became unsustainable. Their organic presence was limited to a few branded terms and a neglected corporate blog.

## Our Approach

We implemented a three-pillar strategy using the Astro SEO Theme infrastructure:

### 1. Programmatic SEO Architecture

We restructured their resource hub into a programmatic structure that targeted thousands of mid-tail and long-tail B2B queries. By leveraging Astro's SSG capabilities, we generated thousands of fast-loading pages that satisfied user intent without sacrificing performance.

### 2. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

Knowing that AI search engines (like Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews) were becoming a primary discovery channel for enterprise software, we formatted their content to include explicit definitions, data tables, and structured FAQs.

### 3. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

We implemented rigorous Schema.org markup (JSON-LD) across the entire domain, specifically targeting `FAQPage`, `SoftwareApplication`, and `Article` schemas.

## The Results

Within 6 months, the results exceeded all expectations:

- **+300% Increase in Organic Traffic**: Non-branded search became their top acquisition channel.
- **-40% Reduction in CAC**: By replacing paid clicks with organic traffic, the blended CAC dropped significantly.
- **Featured Snippet Domination**: TechCorp secured the Featured Snippet for over 200 high-intent industry queries.
